About Workflows

Workflow is a Common Core Service (CCS) within the CPD platform. It gives customers a centralized tool to build, configure, launch, and manage workflows for their business processes.

A workflow is a combination of prescribed people and processes in order to change something, create something new, or alert users. The framework connects across data governance, data quality remediation, data access requests, service requests, and general requests.
