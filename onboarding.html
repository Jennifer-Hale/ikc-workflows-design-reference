The initiative

Customers need a centralized tool for workflows within the CPD platform to build, configure, launch, and manage their business processes.

Workflow is a Common Core Service (CCS): a shared, reusable service across the platform. It connects to several domains:

  • Service requests
  • Data governance
  • Data access and requests
  • General requests
  • Open scale
  • Data Quality remediation

What is a workflow?

A combination of prescribed people and processes in order to change something, create something new, or alert users.

Key features

  • Workflow configuration and management
  • Task inbox and system notifications
  • Task management and metrics

Why workflow matters

  • Establish and streamline processes
  • Gain visibility into business processes
  • Identify and respond to process issues
  • Improve business operation efficiency

Types of workflows

Human-centric

  • Most or all tasks assigned to humans
  • Requires human involvement
  • Requires human approvals
  • May require humans to create something new

System-centric

  • Most or all tasks done by machine
  • May require little to no human involvement
  • Workflow can trigger machine tasks
  • Machine tasks include creating assets (e.g. reports)

What is Flowable?

An open-source workflow engine used to generate workflow models for business processes such as request-for-approval processes.

How is it used with CPD?

It serves as the workflow engine. It's used to create new types of workflows and to extend or modify the ones that ship out of the box.

Out-of-the-box templates

  • AutoPublish
  • One step approval and one review step
  • Two step approval and one review step

Templates can be used as-is by a Configurator, or customized further by a Workflow Developer.

Who are our users?

Requester

Initiates internal requests to obtain, use, or manage data for project needs.

Data Scientist, Data Analyst

Approver

Reviews and approves data requests to be fulfilled.

Data Steward, Data Analyst

Fulfiller

Fulfills internal data requests for assets they are responsible for.

Data Engineer, System Administrator

Builder

Configures workflow tools so business users can launch and fulfill requests.

App Developer, System Administrator
