H1 2025 Competitor study

UX evaluation of CPD workflows vs. Informatica, Collibra, Ataccama, Alation, and Atlan. Conducted April 2025 by Armando Garza and Jennifer Hale.

Top recommendations Gaps identified IBM differentiators About the study Deliverables

Top recommendations

Four high-impact opportunities surfaced from the competitor comparison, ranked by user value and feasibility.

01

Show request status

Provide requesters an easy view into the status of a running request. 4 of 5 competitors include this (Atlan, Alation, Ataccama, Informatica). Directly requested by Lockheed Martin. Explorations started in Mural and Figma — now shipped in 5.4 SW.

02

Enhance commenting

Allow @mentions, links, file attachments, and notifications outside of CPD. 4 of 5 competitors include this (Alation, Collibra, Informatica, Ataccama). Explorations started in Figma.

03

Visual UI for workflow configuration

Allow configurators to build workflows through a visual interface rather than form-based config. 3 of 5 competitors include this (Collibra, Informatica, Atlan). No IBM explorations started yet.

04

Incorporate generative AI

Bring workflow capabilities into the Data Intelligence Assistant chat experience. 3 of 5 competitors include this (Informatica, Ataccama, Atlan). Growing as IBM expands AI capabilities. Explorations started in Figma.

About the study

Assessed CPD workflow and collaboration features against five market alternatives across three user perspectives: requesters (launching and tracking requests), task fulfillers (interacting with assigned tasks), and workflow builders (configuring and managing workflows). Research methods included competitor documentation review, demo videos, and free product demos.

Useful context for design decisions, especially given Lockheed Martin's recent migration from Collibra to CPD.
